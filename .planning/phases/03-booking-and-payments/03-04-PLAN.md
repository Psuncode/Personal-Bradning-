- `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` (test mode key from Stripe Dashboard)
- `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (from `stripe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/webhooks/stripe`)
- `RESEND_API_KEY` (from Resend Dashboard)
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_PHOTOGRAPHY_URL=http://localhost:3000`

2. In a separate terminal, start Stripe webhook forwarding: `stripe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/webhooks/stripe`

4. Click "Book Now" on the Portrait Session card — verify redirect to `/photography/book?pkg=portrait-session`
5. Verify the Portrait Session package is pre-selected on step 1
6. Click "Continue" — verify step 2 (date picker) appears
7. Select an available weekday — verify step 3 (time slots) appears
8. Select a time slot, click "Continue" — verify step 4 (details form) appears
9. Fill in name and email, click "Proceed to Payment" — verify redirect to Stripe Checkout
10. Complete payment with test card: `4242 4242 4242 4242`, any future expiry, any CVC, any zip
11. Verify redirect to `/photography/book/success` with package name, date/time, and deposit amount shown

13. Verify the email contains an ICS attachment and the booking details
14. Visit `http://localhost:3000/admin` (login if needed) — verify the new booking appears in the bookings table
15. Test cancelled flow: Go back to `/photography/book`, fill out form, proceed to Stripe, click back — verify the "Payment was cancelled" banner appears
