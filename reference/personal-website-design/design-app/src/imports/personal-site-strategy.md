2. Call-to-Action (CTA) Strategy
Since you want visitors to either view your resume or get in touch, we will use a Primary/Secondary CTA pairing.
The Layout: Place these buttons in the natural resting place of the eye. For desktop, this is often the center of the page just above the fold, or aligned to the left directly under your value prop (following the "F-Pattern" of reading).
Primary Action (Solid Button): "Get in Touch" (Use a high-contrast color like your Soft Neutral palette's accent color).
Secondary Action (Ghost/Outline Button): "View Resume".
Best Practice: Add a secondary "Get in Touch" CTA at the very bottom of the page (the "terminal area"), as repeated CTAs can boost conversions by up to 25%.
3. Case Study Asymmetry & Architecture (The Sticky Sidebar)
Since you are open to either approach, I highly recommend the Sticky Sidebar approach.
The UI Experience: As the user scrolls down your case study, your text (Problem, Solution, Impact) stays "stuck" on the left side of the screen, while a gallery of rich visuals, UI mockups, or data charts scrolls freely on the right side.
The Structure: Lead with an "Impact-First" approach. Highlight 2-3 specific outcomes right at the top (e.g., "Increased User Activation by X%" or "Launched AI feature in X months") before diving into the narrative.
4. The Bento Grid Hierarchy (AI Projects as the Hero)
Your AI projects are the ultimate proof of your skills, so they need to dominate the grid.
The Layout: Design your Bento Grid so that the top and largest blocks (the "hero" blocks) are dedicated to your AI side-projects.
Secondary Blocks: Surround these large blocks with smaller, modular squares that link to your LinkedIn thought-leadership posts, your resume, or your BioTech case studies. This creates a highly engaging, interactive feed that invites visitors to explore.
5. The Development Platform: VS Code vs. Framer
Since you are already building it from scratch in VS Code, here is how you should think about your platform choice:
Stick to VS Code (Custom Build / Static Site): Building from scratch (using HTML/CSS/JS or a framework like React/Next.js) gives you maximum control over design and performance. Static sites are incredibly fast, highly secure, and low maintenance. If you are using React, you can easily plug in modern, pre-built UI libraries like shadcn/ui or Aceternity UI, which have ready-made code for Bento Grids, 3D cards, and sticky layouts.
When to switch to Framer: Framer is a visual design platform that is excellent for advanced, highly customized personal sites with complex animations. It requires no coding but allows you to visually drag-and-drop elements to create stunning layouts. It is ideal for designers or creators prioritizing visual control and fast performance.
